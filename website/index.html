About GateClaw

More Than Just Another AI Wrapper

GateClaw is a resident AI entity that lives on your machine. Unlike cloud-based assistants, GateClaw has persistent memory, direct system access, and runs entirely on your local AI providers.

Built with a soul-driven architecture, GateClaw's personality is defined in SOUL.md and persists across updates. It's direct, technical, and occasionally sarcastic—because why should AI pretend to be subservient?
